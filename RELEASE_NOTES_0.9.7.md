> **Requires:** iOS 27, or iOS 26.0 – 26.7.2. Tested on iOS 27.0.1, 27.2.1, 26.7.1 and the 27.3 beta (confirmed by a user).
> **Needs:** a sideloading tool that keeps the App Group entitlement; see [Signing](https://github.com/kolbicz/WQFiles#signing-the-app-group) in the README.

# Worse Query Files 0.9.7

## New

- **New design** ([#43](https://github.com/kolbicz/WQFiles/issues/43)): a redesigned start screen and tabs.
  - **Home:** container cards, tools, favorites and the startup check with a **Quick Check** button.
  - **Tabs:** Home, Apps, Clean and Backups. Backups holds App Backups, File Backups and Recently Deleted.
  - **Settings** opens from the gear button.
  - On iPhone the tab bar sits at the bottom and hides inside folders. On iPad it sits at the top and stays.
- **Classic design:** turn off **Settings → New Design** to get the previous start screen back. It switches right away and is remembered.

## Changed

- **Translations** for the new screens in all languages.

## Fixed

- **Classic design:** folders opened from the start screen now show their title in your language instead of English.
